# Twitter/X - AI Studio Community Strategy

## Purpose
Leverage Twitter/X for short, impactful updates to engage with the tech and AI community using well-known hashtags for discoverability.

---

## Engagement Plan:

### Actions:
1. **Create a Dedicated Handle**:
   - Example: @AIstudioMilano.
2. **Tweet Regular Updates**:
   - Showcase progress updates, GitHub highlights, and community calls.
   - Retweet relevant posts from popular AI contributors.
   - Use AI-specific hashtags: `#ArtificialIntelligence`, `#OpenAI`, `#AIstartup`.
3. **Host Spaces**:
   - Organize Twitter Spaces to drive real-time interactions about AI projects.
4. **Engage with Followers**:
   - Reply to tweets mentioning AI Studio.
   - Follow and engage with active AI developers and thought leaders.

---

## Automation:
- **Scheduled Tweets**:
  - Use social media bots to queue and post updates consistently.
- **Analytics Reports**:
  - Track performance metadata weekly (impressions, engagements).

---

## Expected Outcomes:
- Boosted visibility via retweets.
- Drive traffic to GitHub and product pages.
