# AI Studio Accademia Milano — Community Map

> Purpose: Maps the distributed digital and physical presence strategy for AI Studio, defining how people discover, interact, and become clients or collaborators.
> Owner Agent: Francesca (Delivery / Outreach)
> Status: active

## Goal

Create a distributed digital presence where:
- people discover AI Studio
- interact with AI systems
- submit ideas and problems
- receive tangible outputs
- become recurring clients or collaborators

## Core Principle

Every community interaction must eventually produce a tangible output:
deployed software, automation, website, app, AI workflow, public demo, GitHub repo, QR code, or live agent.

No abstract consulting.

## Community Categories

### 1. Builder Communities

| Platform | Purpose | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub | credibility, open-source | repos, APIs, SaaS MVPs |
| Hacker News | technical visibility | experiments, launches |
| Indie Hackers | product builders | MVPs, demos |
| Product Hunt | launches, discovery | users, visibility |
| Reddit (r/programming, etc.) | pain points, trends | SaaS ideas, leads |
| Discord (tech) | collaborators, builders | live demos, collaborative builds |
| X/Twitter | viral demos, founder positioning | audience, inbound requests |

### 2. Professional Communities (B2B Pain)

Targets: lawyers, accountants, architects, doctors, insurance professionals, restaurants, freelancers

| Platform | Outputs |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | automations, booking systems, AI assistants |
| WhatsApp / Telegram groups | client bots, intake systems |
| Local Milan communities | dashboards, document parsers |

### 3. Creative Communities

| Platform | Outputs |
|---|---|
| Instagram | AI art tools, live coding |
| TikTok | generative installations, AI DJs |
| YouTube | educational funnel, live builds |

### 4. Local Physical Communities

Locations: Milan universities, coworkings, bars, maker spaces, design schools

Outputs: hackathons, workshops, prototypes, local AI products

## Interaction Funnel

```
Stage 1 — Discovery    (demo, reel, before/after → Instagram, TikTok, X, LinkedIn)
Stage 2 — Engagement   (comments, DMs, AI terminal, Discord bot)
Stage 3 — Intake       (Stacy collects: problem, files, links, voice notes, goals)
Stage 4 — Specification (Gianni generates: spec, architecture, pricing, timing)
Stage 5 — Build        (Chiara + agents: code, test, deploy, generate assets)
Stage 6 — Delivery     (Francesca delivers: live URL, demo, QR code, repo, automation)
```
