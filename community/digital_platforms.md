# Digital Communities and Platforms — Operational List

> Purpose: Operational reference for each digital platform used by AI Studio — purpose, automation strategy, and expected outputs.
> Owner Agent: Francesca (Delivery / Outreach)
> Status: active

## Platform Matrix

| Platform | Purpose | Automation | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| GitHub | source code, credibility, templates | repo generation, issue triage, AI coding agents | repos, APIs, templates, deploy pipelines |
| LinkedIn | B2B acquisition, professional trust | AI-generated posts, auto case studies, AI outreach drafts | booked calls, automation contracts, business apps |
| Discord | live community, AI assistant interaction | AI bots, voice rooms, coding assistants, project generators | live demos, AI-generated projects, collaborative builds |
| Reddit | discover pain points, observe trends | subreddit monitoring, lead extraction, summarization | SaaS ideas, automation opportunities, market intelligence |
| X / Twitter | visibility, founder positioning, viral demos | auto-thread generation, launch announcements | audience, collaborations, inbound requests |
| Product Hunt | launches, credibility, discovery | launch asset generation, changelog generation | users, visibility, feedback |
| Instagram | visual storytelling, AI atelier identity | AI-generated reels, timelapses, build visualizations | local awareness, creative audience, physical visitors |
| Telegram | lightweight AI interactions, notifications | AI agents, deployment notifications, customer bots | subscriptions, automation services |
| WhatsApp | mass adoption, business interactions | booking agents, support bots, intake systems | clients, automations, AI assistants |
| YouTube | authority, documentation, live builds | AI summaries, subtitles, chapter generation | educational funnel, long-term discovery |
