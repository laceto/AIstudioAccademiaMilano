# AI Risk Management Team — Architecture

# Goal

Build an AI-native risk management structure capable of:
- identifying risks,
- quantifying risks,
- monitoring systems,
- preventing failures,
- protecting capital,
- protecting reputation,
- protecting operations.

---

# Why It Matters

Most AI projects fail because:
- no governance,
- no monitoring,
- no operational controls,
- no financial discipline,
- no security review.

AI Studio must behave like:
- an engineering company,
- a financial operator,
- a risk-aware production system.
