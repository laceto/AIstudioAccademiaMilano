# AI Studio — Capital and Compensation Principles

# Foundational Rule

The founder must be compensated.

Why?

Because the founder absorbs:
- uncertainty,
- execution pressure,
- financial exposure,
- reputational exposure,
- technological experimentation.

---

# Anti-Hobby Principle

AI Studio is not:
- a hobby project,
- an experimental toy,
- a pure research initiative.

It is:
- an economic machine,
- a production engine,
- a value generation system.

---

# Compensation Philosophy

Revenue should progressively move toward:
- recurring subscriptions,
- retained AI operations,
- reusable templates,
- deployable infrastructure,
- scalable systems.

---

# Capital Allocation Priorities

## Priority 1
Infrastructure:
- compute

## Priority 2
