### Breakfast rotation

- **Oatmeal with stewed apple, walnuts and cinnamon** _(Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday)_ — Steel-cut oats simmered with milk, finished with stewed apple, walnuts and a dusting of cinnamon.
- **Whole-grain toast with ricotta, olive oil and tomato** _(Thursday, Friday)_ — A thick slice of whole-grain bread, fresh ricotta, good olive oil and ripe tomato slices, finished with salt and oregano.
- **Polenta with poached egg and sautéed greens** _(Saturday, Sunday)_ — Creamy polenta topped with sautéed cavolo nero, a poached egg and a glug of olive oil.
