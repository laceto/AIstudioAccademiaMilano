### Breakfast rotation

- **Greek yoghurt with strawberries, walnuts and honey** _(Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday)_ — Plain Greek yoghurt topped with sliced strawberries, a handful of walnuts and a drizzle of honey.
- **Whole-grain toast with ricotta, olive oil and tomato** _(Thursday, Friday)_ — A thick slice of whole-grain bread, fresh ricotta, good olive oil and ripe tomato slices, finished with salt and oregano.
- **Spanish-style tomato bread with soft-boiled egg** _(Saturday, Sunday)_ — Toasted sourdough rubbed with garlic and ripe tomato, topped with a 6-minute egg and flaky salt.
