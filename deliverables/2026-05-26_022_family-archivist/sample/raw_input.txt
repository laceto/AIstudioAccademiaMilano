Um so it was the summer of 1974, you know, I was twenty three years old and my brother Paolo he he wanted to go to Naples to visit our aunt, and we had this beat-up Fiat 500, it was sort of yellow but mostly rust, and we left Milano on a Friday morning. The thing is the the windows wouldn't roll up properly, they were stuck halfway, and basically we hit rain near Bologna and we got soaked, I mean really soaked, but we kept driving because we were young and stupid and we thought we would dry out. Near Rome we had a flat tire, eh, and Paolo didn't know how to change it and I didn't know either but we figured it out together, took us two hours on the side of the autostrada in the sun. And um when we got to Naples that evening our aunt she opened the door and she just laughed at us, we looked terrible, sunburned and damp and exhausted, but she had made ragù, you know, the real Neapolitan kind that simmers for six hours, and we ate three plates each and slept until noon the next day. I'll never forget that smell of the ragù when she opened the door.
