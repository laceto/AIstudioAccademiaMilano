# Staff Assignments

## Overview
The following are role-based staff assignments to implement the Launch Plan for AI Studio Accademia Milano.

### Assignments:
1. **Luigi (Fondatore)**
   - Approve overall vision and milestones for the startup launch.
   - Handle fundraising events and legal entity registration.

2. **Marco (Il Banchiere)**
   - Finalize the business bank account setup.
   - Oversee the rental agreements and cost management.

3. **Gianni (Il Tecnico)**
   - Purchase and install dispensers.
   - Coordinate with vendors for high-tech hardware.

4. **Chiara (La Designer)**
   - Build interactive user interfaces for the dispensers.
   - Prepare marketing materials and branding kits.

5. **Francesca (La Venditrice)**
   - Onboard businesses interested in AI dispensers.
   - Drive customer attraction strategies.

6. **Stacy (AI Braccio Destro)**
   - Automate team tracking and report work progress.
   - Manage AI dispenser workflows for the first prototypes.

---
The staff must coordinate and report updates weekly to Luigi.
